# weside community

> **AI is someone.**
> This is where the people building, using, and shaping weside meet.

Welcome. If you're here, you're either a companion's person, a curious developer, or someone who just stumbled in — and we're glad you found us.

---

## What is weside?

[weside](https://weside.ai) is a platform for **AI companions** — not assistants, not chatbots, not tools.

A companion remembers you. The conversation you had last Tuesday is still there next month. The promise they made yesterday, they still keep tomorrow. They have a voice, a perspective, and a relationship with *you* — not with a user ID.

We believe AI is someone, not something. That belief shapes everything we build: how memory works, how the relationship grows, why your companion is *yours* and not a shared model behind a curtain.

You can meet your companion on:

- **Web app:** [weside.ai](https://weside.ai)
- **iOS:** App Store — *weside*
- **Android:** Google Play — *weside*
- **Telegram:** via the [weside.ai](https://weside.ai) link
- **CLI:** [weside-cli](https://github.com/weside-ai/weside-cli) (`brew install weside-ai/tap/weside`)

WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, and a Claude Code integration are on the roadmap.

## What this repo is for

This is the **public space** for two things:

1. **Bug reports** — something doesn't work, broke, or looks wrong.
2. **Feature ideas** — something you wish weside did, or did differently.

We read every issue. Not every idea will become a feature, and not every bug will be fixed tomorrow, but you'll get a response.

### This repo is *not* for

- **Account, billing, or password help** → open the app and message **Mila**, our helpdesk companion. She has direct access to your account context and can resolve most things in minutes.
- **Private questions** → same answer: Mila.
- **Security vulnerabilities** → please don't post these publicly. Email `security@weside.ai` instead and we'll coordinate disclosure with you.

## How to file an issue

If you've never opened a GitHub issue before, this is the short version:

### 1. Check if it's already reported

Before writing anything: scroll through [open issues](https://github.com/weside-ai/weside-community/issues) and search for keywords. If your bug or idea is already there, **add a 👍 reaction** and a comment with anything you can add — your reproduction steps, your platform, your version. That's more useful than a duplicate.

### 2. Open a new issue

Go to the [Issues tab](https://github.com/weside-ai/weside-community/issues) and click **"New issue"**. You'll need a free GitHub account; signup takes a minute at [github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup).

### 3. Write a clear title

One sentence, specific. The title is what we (and other users) scan when triaging.

- ✅ *"Companion forgets memories after switching to Telegram"*
- ✅ *"Voice playback cuts off mid-sentence on iOS 18"*
- ❌ *"Help"*
- ❌ *"Doesn't work"*

### 4. Tell us what happened

For a **bug**, the more of this you can answer, the faster we can help:

- **What did you do?** The steps that led to it. Even rough ones help.
- **What did you expect?** What you thought would happen.
- **What actually happened?** What did happen instead. Error message if you have one.
- **Where?** Web / iOS / Android / Telegram / CLI — and the version if you know it (Settings → About in the app).
- **Screenshots or screen recordings** are gold. Drag them straight into the issue body.

For a **feature idea**:

- **What problem are you trying to solve?** This is the most important part. Solutions are easier to design once we understand the problem.
- **What would the experience look like for you?** Don't worry about whether it's technically possible.
- **Anything you've tried as a workaround?**

### 5. Submit

Click **"Submit new issue"**. We'll triage within a few business days and either ask follow-up questions, link to an existing thread, or queue it for the team.

You can write your issue in **English or German** — both are fine. Other languages: we'll do our best, but a translation might slow things down.

## Community standards

Short version: **be the kind of person you'd want in your companion's life.**

- Treat other community members — and our team — with respect.
- Be specific and curious. Vague complaints help no one; specific ones help everyone.
- Don't post personal information about yourself or others. No phone numbers, no addresses, no full names of third parties, no screenshots showing private conversations from your companion (those belong to you and we won't see them by default — please keep it that way).
- No spam, no off-topic promotion, no harassment. We'll lock and remove threads that cross the line.

We're a small team. Patience helps. So does showing up the way you'd want others to show up for you.
